Chenxin Liu 刘辰昕

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at IIIS, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Jianyu Chen. I received my bachelor's degree from the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Tsinghua University.

My research focuses on humanoid robot learning, with an emphasis on whole-body control and physical interaction. I am interested in how experience can help bridge the gap between what robots learn and what they can reliably do. My goal is to develop and validate robotic systems that can perform a broad range of tasks reliably in the real world.

For research discussions or potential collaborations, feel free to reach me at liu-cx21@mails.tsinghua.edu.cn.
